Collaborate

Conscientia is seeking skilled, professional and reliable collaborators who can contribute to the project’s artistic and technical development.

Compensation is available for suitable collaborations. Scope, terms and rates are negotiable and will be discussed individually.

Currently seeking

Vocalist

A versatile vocalist capable of powerful screams and expressive clean vocals, with controlled high-register singing, strong melodic phrasing and a broad emotional range.

Drummer

A technically capable drummer who can contribute creatively to the composition, arrangement and development of complex drum parts.

Visual Design

Assistance with visual identity, artwork, symbols and digital material that can translate Conscientia’s philosophical character into a coherent visual language.

Mixing & Mastering

An experienced engineer capable of balancing heavy, progressive and atmospheric elements while preserving clarity, dynamics and impact.

Promotion

A promotional collaborator or partner who can contribute to strategy, content, outreach and release campaigns with a professional and long-term approach.
