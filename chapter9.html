One man, one journey. He is now at peace with himself, for he neither knows nor believes that he knows what is truly good. He no longer needs to battle the unforeseeable future in the hope of bringing himself into harmony with the “truths” he pursued in his former state. Yet he remains within the eternal cycle — now carrying with him an awareness of his own weaknesses. One man is being mended.
